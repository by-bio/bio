![The original Link Inc. story page introducing the fictional organization and Miss Link](/projects/turnips-exchange/link-brand/story-page.jpg)

The Deserted Island Getaway Package doesn’t sound unfamiliar to you, right? Good! Because this is where our story begins.

It was not too long ago when Nook Inc. released this package. People from all over the world got super excited since the first moment of the first day. We have had enough of this busy, boring, tedious life. We need something different, something refreshing, something cheering and relaxing. A stimulation or a push, new friends, running, jumping, climbing, swimming... A new start! And that’s why everyone loves the Getaway Package. What a wonderful opportunity to get away for a moment!

## New islands

In the Southern Hemisphere or the Northern Hemisphere, countless deserted islands were awakened for their new springs. The new islanders came to their islands and couldn’t wait to start to create their own dream worlds. From placing the first tent, to inviting new villagers; from setting up a campfire, to DIYing and building their own tools and houses; from fishing and catching bugs, to weeding and planting trees and flowers; upgrading resident service and building museum... Everything was so energizing and everyone was so vitalized. The islands became their most precious paradise.

## Finding one another

But the journey didn’t end with living on their own islands alone. Thanks to Dodo Airlines, islanders were able to travel to random islands with just 1 Nook Mile Ticket. During the trips, they met and invited new villagers, found new resources and plants and migrated them back to home. And guess what? They found each other! It turns out that they were always just a flight away. And they had never talked to and visited each other. What a shame! Well, it’s never too late to start. Since then, more and more flights have been created between the islanders. They visited, shared, and traded. They found out the interests they had in common. Friendships had been established. They enjoyed the sincere environment created by each and every one of them. Adventuring together is so much better than being alone!

The connected groups opened a new level of fun. Flea markets, shooting stars party, tarantula catching competition, going to KK’s concerts... Oh, and of course, exchanging turnips price information! So many different activities. And the groups grew larger and larger and got connected and merged with each other and kept expanding. Everyone was sharing resources and information to more islanders; everyone was inviting each other to visit their islands; everyone was adding more fun to the community. Yes, a community! A community where all members are nice and friendly and everyone is warmly welcomed to join. Couldn’t imagine how much fun they must be missing for those who are still alone out there and haven’t joined.

![Miss Link wearing a yellow Link uniform and cap inside Animal Crossing](/projects/turnips-exchange/link-brand/miss-link-portrait.jpg)

## Link Inc.

To get the activities in the community more organised, the islanders who pioneered in traveling around and organizing groups agreed that it’s time to form a dedicated organisation to help people in the community. So, they founded Link Inc.

The purpose of Link Inc. is to provide organised and standardised services to everyone in the community so that they can worry less and enjoy more, to keep all members connected and to make links to all isolated islands. Link Inc. has another important mission. Friendliness and trustworthiness are the most important characteristics of the community. It is the sincereness of every individual member that helped to re-establish connection in the once disconnected world. Link Inc. strives to maintain and remind everyone of those characteristics, and to guard and protect the community and everyone here.

## Link Airport

After months of planning and preparation, Link Inc. is finally ready to launch its first project: Link Airport. Link Airport is equipped with the latest technology for sharing information and organising flights. In the Terminals with aggregated island and flight information, people can easily find interesting islands to tour and resourceful islands to trade. Automated queuing and boarding system make sure everyone gets to their destination orderly and safely. Chatting space in the lounge allows everyone to freely communicate and make friends with people from all over the world.

> No one should be left alone.

Link Airport is the first planned attempt to more effectively connect islanders and Link Inc. will continue to launch new projects to make that wish a reality.
